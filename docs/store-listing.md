## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Tired of political spam texts flooding your inbox every election season? PoliticalTextKiller detects and removes unwanted political text messages automatically — all processing happens on your device.

**How it works:**
- Keyword-based detection catches known political spam patterns (fundraising, PACs, donation links)
- On-device ML classifier (TensorFlow Lite) catches messages that slip past keywords with 90%+ precision
- Notification killer silences spam alerts in real time
- Optional Shizuku Power Mode deletes spam silently without making PTK your default SMS app

**Key features:**
- Inbox Scan & Purge: Scan your entire SMS inbox and bulk-remove detected political spam
- Kill Log: Full audit trail of every message silently deleted — verify nothing important was caught
- Restore: One-tap restore any message back to your inbox and whitelist the sender
- Vault: Quarantine zone for review before permanent deletion
- Analytics: Track how many messages have been killed over time
- Contact whitelist: Messages from your contacts are never flagged

**Privacy first:**
No data ever leaves your device. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no ads. The ML model runs locally. The only network call is to Google Play for subscription verification.

**Subscription:**
Free 30-day trial. $0.99/month after trial ends. Cancel anytime from Google Play.

**Permissions explained:**
- SMS: Read and delete political spam from your inbox
- Notifications: Silence spam notifications in real time
- Contacts: Whitelist messages from people you know

**Requirements:**
- Android 12+ (API 31)
- Optional: Shizuku app for silent deletion without default SMS swap
